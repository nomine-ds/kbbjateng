# Prompt 5: SECTION 4 KASUS KUNCI

**Aspect ratio:** 16:9
**Konteks editorial:** Empat kasus kunci (Sudiyono, siswa Surakarta, Sri Rahayu, Sedulur Sikep). Kursi kosong melambangkan kehadiran yang tidak diakui oleh negara.

---

## Prompt (English)

Four identical wooden chairs arranged in a row, viewed from slightly above and to the side. On each chair rests a single document, slightly different from the others. Soft directional light from the left casts gentle shadows. The chairs are empty, the people who should be sitting there are absent. Painterly editorial illustration, muted palette of warm grey, dusty rose, and deep charcoal. Subtle grain, oil-on-canvas texture. No text, no people, no photorealism. 16:9 aspect ratio.

## Negative prompt

text, papers with readable content, people, humans, faces, violence, photorealism, churches, mosques

---

## Cara pakai

1. Buka Midjourney / DALL-E 3 / Leonardo.ai / Stable Diffusion
2. Copy prompt di atas (tanpa tanda kutip pembuka-penutup)
3. Paste ke kolom prompt tool tersebut
4. Paste negative prompt ke kolom negative (khusus Stable Diffusion / Leonardo)
5. Generate 4-6 variasi, pilih yang terbaik
6. Simpan sebagai WebP kualitas 85% di `web/assets/img/05-kasus.webp`
7. Tambah caption di bawah gambar: "Ilustrasi dibuat dengan bantuan AI. Bukan foto kejadian sebenarnya."

- Jangan pernah tambah wajah, orang nyata, atau simbol agama tertentu
